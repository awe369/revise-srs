Make cloze cards on any website. Choose where your cards are saved — each person's cards go to their own storage.

Anki

Also save every card in Anki, with the same look (note type ClozeForge). Making, editing or deleting a card here updates Anki too — whenever Anki is open on this computer.

  1. In Anki: Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons… → code 2055492159 (AnkiConnect) → restart Anki.
  2. Keep Anki open and click Connect Anki below. Anki asks once “Allow?” → click Yes.

For PDFs saved on this computer: chrome://extensions → ClozeForge → Details → turn on Allow access to file URLs.

How to use

  1. Select text on any page → click the […] Cloze button, right-click → Make cloze card, or press Alt+Shift+C.
  2. Click words in the preview to hide them. Each number (c1, c2…) becomes its own review.
  3. Press Ctrl+Enter to save. Review on the site (sign in there with the same Google account) — open a card's source to quiz yourself on the original page.
