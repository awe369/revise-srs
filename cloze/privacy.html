[…] ClozeForge privacy

ClozeForge (the Chrome extension and this review site) has no server and no database of its own.

  • Google Drive: when you sign in with Google, your cards are saved to ClozeForge/cloze-cards.json in your Google Drive. The app asks only for the drive.file permission, so it can see only the files it created itself — never the rest of your Drive.
  • GitHub (optional): if you choose GitHub instead, cards are saved to a repository you own, using a token you create.
  • Sign-in tokens stay in your browser / extension storage and are sent only to Google or GitHub.
  • The extension reads the text you select on a page only when you make a card, and stores it in your card together with the page address.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or sent anywhere else. No analytics, no ads.

To delete everything: delete the ClozeForge folder in your Google Drive, and remove the app at myaccount.google.com/connections.
